About JTrack

Operational reporting should shorten the distance between a question and a responsible decision.

JTrack is a focused reporting workspace for teams managing student applications, offers, enrollment, and related operational work.

It keeps the source workflow familiar—Excel, SQLite, or SharePoint—while creating a stable place to monitor changes and examine detail. Leaders and managers see the reports relevant to their work; each report keeps the chart and underlying rows together.

What we value

Clarity
Specific labels, visible data states, and no decorative noise competing with the numbers.
Continuity
A team can improve its reporting practice without replacing the systems that already hold its data.
Control
Role boundaries, source metadata, and exportable tables keep the workflow accountable.
